What the app does

Everything you need on the course.

Designed to be used with one hand between shots: few taps, screens readable in the sun.

TEEStart & hole end

One tap at the tee, one tap on the green

“Start” begins measuring once GPS has settled; “End of hole” freezes your exact position on the green, for a reliable as-the-crow-flies distance.

SHOTDistance per shot

Every shot, tied to its club

Pick your club, tap “Next shot”: the shot distance is measured by GPS and saved for your per-club statistics.

WALKDistance walked

What you really walked

GPS is cross-checked with the iPhone pedometer to filter out signal noise. In our field tests the gap with an Apple Watch was only a few meters over more than 9 km (5.7 mi). Includes a “phone on the cart” option and a golf cart option.

SCOREScorecard

Several players, Stableford, PDF export

Course Handicap and Stableford calculated for each player, with the tees of your choice. Share the final card or print it as a PDF.

STATSStatistics

Your averages, club by club

Average distance per club, putts per hole, round history: your game in numbers, over the period you choose.

SHAREDShared courses

Courses added by the community

Courses (holes, tees, rating and slope) are added and shared by players. Yours isn't there yet? Create it in a few minutes and everyone benefits. The nearest course is suggested automatically when you open the app.
