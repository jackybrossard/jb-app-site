1. Logging in

When you first launch the app, it asks you to log in with an email address and a password.

  • You already have an account: enter your email and password, then tap “Log in”.
  • Create an account: switch to “Create an account” if you don't have credentials yet.
  • Forgot password: tap the dedicated link to receive a reset email.
💡Once logged in, you stay logged in for about 1 hour without re-entering your credentials. After that, you need to log in again.

Then, on every tab, a small 👤 icon at the top left lets you see your email and log out at any time, without going through the Settings tab.

2. Dashboard (Home)

The first tab gives an overview of your finances.

Backup reminder

If no JSON backup has been made for 8 days (or ever), an orange banner appears at the top with a “Save” button — one tap is enough to start the export and share it (see the Settings section).

Upcoming due dates

If a recurring transaction is due within the next 8 days (or is overdue), it appears here: 🔴 overdue, 🟠 in 3 days or less, 🟡 beyond.

Bank / Scope

A row of bubbles filters the figures displayed below: “All” or one or more banks. Tap to select; tap again to deselect.

💡Bank groups (🔗) are not offered here — you will find them in the Payee ranking below and in the Budget tab.

Global balance and verified balance

  • Global balance: opening balance + all recorded transactions.
  • Verified balance: opening balance + only the transactions you have ticked as verified.

Payee ranking

Top 10 of the selected payees (see Settings), compared over 3 years. The “Rank by” selector lets you choose which of the 3 years the ranking is based on (by default the current year, except in January, when the app automatically starts on the previous year, for lack of enough data). A bank/group selector lets you restrict the ranking to a specific scope. The “Export ranking (PDF)” button generates a document ready to share, with the ranking year indicated. This document lists the top 30.

Search a payee

Type a few letters to find a payee and see the latest transaction and the total spending per year.

Balance by bank

Balance details (total and verified) for each bank shown in the scope selected above.

3. Transactions (Txns)

This tab lists all your transactions, with search, filters and quick actions.

Search and filter

The search bar filters by payee, category or notes. The ⊚ icon opens a dedicated filter sheet: period (all / by month / by year / custom period), bank, budget code, payment method, category, verification status, attachment.

💡The “Clear all filters” button resets everything in one tap.

A transaction card

  • The circle on the left (⬜/✅) ticks or unticks the transaction as “verified”.
  • The amount is red (expense) or green (income).
  • The three icons under the line: pencil (edit), two sheets (duplicate), trash (delete).

Duplicating a transaction

The “duplicate” icon offers two options:

  • In Transactions: creates an editable copy of the transaction (same date, same amount), to adjust afterwards.
  • In Recurring: opens the Recurring form pre-filled with the transaction's details — handy for turning a one-off expense into a recurring subscription.
💡It is the Notes field of the transaction (not that of the recurring transaction) that becomes the name of the recurring transaction when you duplicate “In Recurring” — handy if the payee alone doesn't distinguish the transaction. (See 8. Tips and advice)

Multiple selection

The “Select” button lets you tick several transactions to delete them at once.

Exporting

In the filter sheet, two buttons let you export the current filtered selection: “Generate PDF” or “Export as CSV”.

4. Adding a transaction

The “+” button at the top opens a form: date (today by default), payee, category, bank and method, amount (debit or credit), budget code, notes, documents. Only bank and method are required.

Every field with a search also lets you create a new item on the fly if the one you're looking for doesn't exist yet.

💡In the search area, type the label: if it exists, it appears in the list. Otherwise, scroll to the bottom of the list: a “Create a new item” area, with its own “Name…” field, is waiting for you (the text typed in the search is not carried over automatically).

Documents

MesFinances lets you keep receipts or important documents as PDFs. You can also take a photo or pick one from your library.

💡The Document area appears if the threshold set in the Settings tab is reached.

5. Recurring (Recur.)

This tab manages the transactions that come back regularly: subscriptions, salary, rent, etc.

Alert banner

At the top, a summary of the due dates that are close or overdue, over the next 8 days.

A recurring card

Each card shows the name, the frequency (monthly, bimonthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, or fixed date), the status (active/inactive), the amount and the next due date, calculated automatically.

  • Activate / Deactivate: an inactive recurring transaction no longer appears in the alerts or in the due dates.
  • Edit: opens the edit form.
  • Duplicate: creates an inactive copy, handy for a similar subscription.
  • Record: immediately creates the matching transaction on the scheduled date, and moves the next due date forward.
  • Trash (top right of the card, or swipe left): deletes permanently.
💡For a monthly frequency, choose the day of the month (or “end of month”). For cycles (bimonthly, quarterly, half-yearly), also specify a reference month that determines the right rhythm. For a fixed date or an annual frequency, simply choose the date.

6. Budget

Tracking of your budgeted expenses.

Period

Switch between “By month” and “By year”.

Banks

Filter by bank or by bank group (🔗 icon, selects the whole group in one tap). The breakdown by category (which you can show or hide) displays how expenses are split across each code's categories.

Summary cards

“Selected budgets” totals the selected budget codes (see Settings) for the chosen scope.

Budget code card

The amount you enter must be the monthly budget — in “By year” mode, the app displays the value multiplied by 12.

  • Top line: spending for the current period, compared with the previous period, with the percentage change.
  • “Budget” line: informational total for the period, with the usage percentage (green/orange/red depending on the overrun).
  • “Monthly budget” field: this is where you enter or edit the budget — always as a monthly value.
💡A bank group (🔗 icon) lets you consolidate several banks (in the sense of the MesFinances app). Such a group is often made up of your main bank, possibly combined with a deferred-debit credit card and a wallet (Cash).
💡Selected budgets let you manage your budget properly without being cluttered by purely technical ones (internal entries between banks).

7. Settings

Account

Shows your email and lets you log out or delete your account.

⚠️A confirmation by typing the word DELETE is required before any deletion, to avoid mistakes.

You can manage your subscription and read the privacy policy.

Data management

💾 JSON backup — Exports all of your data (banks, transactions, recurring transactions, etc.) to a file you can save wherever you like (Files, email, etc.). Restoring re-imports a backup file.

⚠️Restoring REPLACES all of your current data. This action cannot be undone — always make a recent backup before restoring.

📥 CSV import — Imports a CSV file (for example exported from Numbers or Excel, or exported by the app itself). Recognized columns: DATE, BENEFICIAIRE (payee), NATURE (category), BANQUE (bank), MOYEN (payment method), DEBIT, CREDIT, CODE (budget code). The column headers must be written exactly like this, in capitals. Dates can be DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD. Unknown values (payee, category, bank, method, code) are created automatically. Two modes: “Add” (completes your existing data) or “Replace” (erases everything before importing).

📦 Archiving / Deletion — Lets you export to CSV and then delete the transactions of one year (or all years) and/or of a given bank — the bank's opening balance is recalculated automatically so that the current balance doesn't change. The “Danger zone” also lets you delete all the transactions of a bank directly, without exporting.

📎 Attached document — Fill in the Threshold: from that amount, the app will offer to attach a document (photo or PDF) to the transaction. Leave at 0 to disable.

💡For important purchases, the receipt can be easily found again using the various search tools.

💱 Currency — Choose the currency used to display all amounts in the app (balances, transactions, budget, exports). The price of the Premium subscription is managed separately by the App Store, in your local currency.

Reference data

Each table (Banks, Bank groups, Payees, Categories, Payment methods, Budget codes) has its own screen: search, add, edit (swipe left or tap the row), delete (swipe left, then “Delete”). A confirmation is requested before the deletion becomes permanent.

💡On the screen for entering a new bank, you need to fill in the opening balance; this lets you match your actual bank balance.

🔒 Selection lock — For Payees and Budget codes, a checkbox determines whether they appear in the Dashboard ranking or in the Budget. This selection is locked by default (🔒) to avoid accidental changes — tap “🔓 Edit selection” to unlock it, tick/untick, then “🔒 Done”.

Merging duplicates

On the 5 reference tables, the “🔀 Merge” button lets you combine duplicates: tick the entries to merge, choose which one to keep (the target), and confirm. All the transactions and recurring transactions concerned are automatically attached to the target, and the duplicates are deleted.

💡For banks, the opening balance of the duplicates is added to that of the target, so that no amount is lost in the merge.

8. Tips and advice

  • Back to top: the small ⬆ arrow at the top right of each screen instantly takes you back to the top of the list.
  • Swipe to act: on most lists, swiping a row to the left offers edit/delete.
  • Pull down: on any list screen, pull down to reload the latest data.
  • Closing the keyboard: a ✕ button appears above the keyboard; on lists, scrolling also closes the keyboard automatically.
  • Sharing: every export (PDF, CSV, JSON) opens the standard iPhone share sheet — choose Mail, Files, AirDrop, Messages, etc.

Duplicating a transaction

💡It is the Notes field of the transaction (not that of the recurring transaction) that becomes the name of the recurring transaction when you duplicate “In Recurring”.
Example: you buy apps on the App Store, so the payee is Apple. In the Notes field you enter the name of the app, and it is automatically carried over as the name of the recurring transaction. That way you can easily remember, a few months later, what the recurring charge is for.

Banks

💡A bank group (🔗 icon) lets you consolidate several banks (in the sense of the MesFinances app). Such a group is often made up of your main bank, possibly combined with a deferred-debit credit card and a wallet (Cash).

Why create a “Deferred-debit card” or “Cash” bank?

With a deferred-debit card, your purchases for the month are charged in a single payment, at the end of the month. If you record each purchase directly on your bank, you will count them twice: once at the time of purchase, and again when the grouped payment is charged.

The solution: create a dedicated bank, for example CARD, and record your purchases on that bank as you go.

At the end of the month, when the grouped payment hits your real bank, record two entries:

  • an expense on your real bank (the total amount charged)
  • an income of the same amount on your CARD bank (to bring it back to zero)
Example: you spent 320 with your card in March, spread over 8 purchases. Each purchase is recorded on the “CARD” bank as you go. On March 31, your real bank charges 320 in one go: you record −320 on your real bank, and +320 on “CARD” to bring it back to zero. The same principle applies to a Cash wallet for your cash spending.

9. If something goes wrong

  • An error message is displayed: read it, it usually indicates precisely what failed (connection, missing data…).
  • Frozen or empty screen when loading: pull down to reload, or fully quit the app and relaunch it.
  • Data that seems to be missing: check the active filters on the screen concerned (⊚ icon on Transactions, bank selector on Budget/Dashboard).
  • Before any destructive operation (JSON restore, replacement through CSV import, archiving): make a JSON backup.
