What the app does

Five screens, one habit.

Each feature below matches a tab in the app (HOME, TXNS, RECUR., BUDGET, SETTINGS), so you can move between the five screens at a glance.

HOMEDashboard

The big picture, instantly

Total and verified balance, by bank or bank group, your top payees, and reminders of upcoming payments. Hide your balances with a single tap on the eye icon.

TXNSTransactions

Every expense, every income

Instant search, precise filters, one-tap reconciliation, and PDF or CSV export of your transactions. Attach a photo of an invoice directly to an expense.

RECUR.Recurring

What comes back, without a thought

Rent, salary, subscriptions: schedule your regular transactions and add them in one tap when due, with automatic alerts.

BUDGETBudget

One limit, one colour, one answer

Track spending by category, compare it with the previous month or year, and spot any overspending at a glance.

SETTINGSSettings

Everything under control, in one place

Account, currency, backups, reference data, privacy and subscription: everything is managed from this one screen, without getting lost in menus. A detailed user guide is also available.
